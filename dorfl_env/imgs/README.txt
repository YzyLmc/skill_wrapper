The coding is formulated according to this:
First digit: bread has pb?
  0: has pb
  1: no pb
Second digit: knife as pb?
  0: has pb
  1: no pb
Third digit: holding knife?
  0: knife in cup
  1: holding knife
  2: knife dropped
Forth digit: pb jar open?
  0: closed
  1: open
Fifth digit: pb jar picked up?
  0: on table
  1: picked up
